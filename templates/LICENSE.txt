Common Paper Legal Agreement Templates
=======================================

All files in this directory ("templates/") are legal agreement templates
originally published by Common Paper (https://commonpaper.com) in the
following open-source repositories under the CommonPaper GitHub
organization (https://github.com/CommonPaper):

- Mutual-NDA
- CSA
- Design-Partner-Agreement
- SLA
- PSA
- DPA
- Software-License-Agreement
- Partnership-Agreement
- BAA
- Pilot-Agreement
- AI-Addendum
